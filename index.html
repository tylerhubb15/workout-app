Log a Workout
Tap Log workout to start a session. Search exercises or filter by muscle group and equipment. Need something not on the list? Tap + Add Custom Exercise to name it and tag a muscle group. Check off each set to start the rest timer.
Three Rep Modes
Each exercise has a 3-way toggle: Target Reps for standard training, RIR to log reps alongside proximity to failure, and ER for effective-rep rest-pause sets. Pick the mode that matches your method.
Effective Reps (ER) Training
ER mode is built for rest-pause training. Set an Ignition Reps target (e.g. 12) and an ER Target (e.g. 20 total). Do your ignition set to near-failure, rest 10–15 seconds, then keep going in short bursts until you hit the total. This accumulates maximum effective reps in minimal time.
PR Tracking
The app tracks your all-time best weight for every exercise. When you check off a set that breaks your record, a 🏆 New PR! toast fires instantly. A gold PR badge sticks to the set row so you can see all your records at a glance.
Build a Training Plan
Go to Plans to build a structured program. Choose your workout days and configure each day exercise-by-exercise. Set one plan as Active — it drives the calendar, the home widget, and your next session's pre-loaded exercises.
RIR Progressive Overload
Enable RIR on a plan to run a 4-week mesocycle (RIR 3 → 2 → 1 → 0). The app reads what you logged last week and automatically adjusts this week's rep targets — no math needed.
Calendar, History & Body Weight
The Calendar shows planned and logged days — tap any date to view or log a workout. History shows every session with volume and PR badges. Track your weight over time in the Body Weight section, visible as a sparkline on the home screen.

Guides

RIR (Reps in Reserve) is a way to measure how hard you're training relative to failure. RIR 3 means you could do 3 more reps; RIR 0 means you're at failure.

A mesocycle is a training block (typically 4 weeks) that uses progressively decreasing RIR to drive hypertrophy:

Week 1RIR 3Easy — build the habit, feel the movements
Week 2RIR 2Moderate — increasing effort
Week 3RIR 1Hard — leaving one rep in the tank
Week 4RIR 0Max effort — true failure, peak stimulus

After week 4, deload or start a new mesocycle with slightly higher loads than week 1. This progressive overload cycle is the foundation of long-term muscle growth.

Tip: When the app shows a target RIR for a set, log the reps you actually hit — the next session's targets auto-adjust based on your performance.

Effective Reps (ER) are the reps performed close to muscular failure — typically the last few reps of a set where the muscle is under maximum tension. These are the reps that drive the most growth.

The ER rest-pause method lets you accumulate a target number of effective reps in one extended set:

Step 1IgnitionDo 12–15 reps to near-failure (your ignition set)
Step 2Rest 10–15sTake a brief rest — just enough to recover 1–2 reps
Step 3Keep goingDo as many reps as possible to failure again
Step 4RepeatContinue until you hit your ER target (e.g. 20 total reps)

Example: ER Target 20 · Ignition 13 reps → rest → 3 reps → rest → 2 → 1 → 1 = 20 total effective reps.

Tip: Set the ER Target in the exercise form. Log each mini-set as a separate set. The target keeps you from stopping too early or going too long.
